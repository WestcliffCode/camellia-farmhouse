Design intent: the sun sits behind the house, so rooms glazed to the back and west (great room, kitchen, dining, game room,
primary suite, back vestibule, foyer via the family room opening) take the direct beams. Rooms glazed only to the street
(office, bedrooms 2 and 3) face away from a setting sun by construction; they are lit by sky glow through the windows and
by their lamps and recessed cans, which is the "soft practical lighting" half of the brief.

No single sun position lights every room: turning the sun west brings it into bedroom 4 (its only window faces the side
of the house, so at the chosen azimuth the sun grazes that wall at 19 degrees) but takes it out of the foyer, the portico
and most of the game room. The chosen azimuth keeps the beams in the rooms the cameras feature. The office has one window,
facing the street, directly away from any sun that lights the back of the house.
Window-by-window check at the chosen azimuth (35 rays per opening from just outside the glass toward the sun): the
sun-facing openings are unobstructed (primary suite 35/35 and 35/35, game room 22/35, 35/35 and 35/35, great room 33/35,
dining windows 26/35, dining slider 28/35, kitchen slider 30/35, bedroom 4 35/35); the few blocked rays hit porch posts,
a tree or a chair on the porch.
