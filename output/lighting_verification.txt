Design intent: the sun sits behind the house, so rooms glazed to the back and west (great room, kitchen, dining, game room,
primary suite, back vestibule, foyer via the family room opening) take the direct beams. Rooms glazed only to the street
(office, bedrooms 2 and 3) face away from a setting sun by construction; they are lit by sky glow through the windows and
by their lamps and recessed cans, which is the "soft practical lighting" half of the brief.
